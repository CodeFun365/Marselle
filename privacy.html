Privacy & trust

Your data, and your patients', stay yours.

Marselle handles a clinic's reputation and its patient list — so trust isn't a feature we bolted on, it's the whole foundation. Here is exactly what we touch, what we never touch, and the standards we hold ourselves to. In plain language, no legalese.

Last updated: August 2026 · Questions? marsellegrow@gmail.com

The four promises

Built for a healthcare owner's standards.

01

No clinical records. Ever.

Marselle never touches charts, treatment notes, diagnoses or medical histories. It works only with a plain contact list your front desk exports and controls, plus public information about your clinic — your Google Business Profile, reviews and local rankings. Nothing clinical enters the system, because it never needs to.

02

We never train on your data.

Your data and your patients' data are never used to train AI models. Marselle runs under zero-data-retention terms with its underlying AI providers, so the content and prompts involved in drafting your messages aren't retained or reused. Your patient list is yours alone — not a training set.

03

Nothing sends without your tap.

Every patient message — win-back texts, review replies, newsletters, Google posts — waits for your explicit approval before it goes out. Nothing is published or sent automatically. And Marselle can't spend a dollar of your money; that capability simply doesn't exist in the system.

04

You hold the keys.

Access is by invitation inside your own accounts and revocable in one click. Your contact data is one-click exportable or deletable at any time. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never handed to anyone but you.

In detail

What Marselle touches — and what it never does.

WHAT MARSELLE WORKS WITH

  • A contact list (names, emails, phone numbers) your front desk exports and controls
  • Recall or last-visit dates, so it knows who has lapsed
  • Your public Google Business Profile, reviews and local rankings
  • Public information about the clinics near you, for competitor watch
  • What you tell it about your clinic, your voice and what a patient is worth to you

WHAT MARSELLE NEVER TOUCHES

  • Clinical charts, treatment notes, diagnoses or medical histories
  • Your practice-management or EMR system's clinical records
  • Payment details, insurance data or financial accounts
  • Purchased or third-party lists — it only ever reaches your own patients
  • Your money — it can't spend, buy or transfer anything

Canadian privacy law

PIPEDA & PHIPA-aware, by design.

Marselle is built to sit comfortably inside the privacy rules Canadian clinics operate under. It stays there not by paperwork, but by how it works: it never handles health records, and it only ever messages your own existing patients.

Existing patients only

Reactivation reaches only people already in your records who chose you once — never bought or scraped lists. This keeps outreach within the consent your patients already gave your clinic.

Identified & opt-out, always

Every message clearly identifies your clinic and includes a simple way to opt out — the standard Canadian anti-spam and privacy expectations require. Consent is respected the moment a patient asks to stop.

Marketing, not custody

Marselle is a marketing partner, not a custodian of health information. Because no clinical data flows through it, the heavy PHIPA custodial obligations stay where they belong — with your practice — and Marselle stays a safe layer on top.

Marselle helps you stay within these rules; it doesn't replace your own legal or regulatory obligations. If your regulator or lawyer needs specifics on our handling, we'll answer directly — marsellegrow@gmail.com.

How your data is held

Protected in transit, in storage, and in access.

Encrypted end to end

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. It moves over secure connections and is stored encrypted — never in the clear.

Least-access by default

Only the people and systems that need access to run your workflows have it, scoped to the minimum required, and every access is revocable in one click.

Delete on request, no friction

Ask us to export or delete your data and it happens — promptly, completely, and with confirmation. Your data leaving is as easy as it arriving.

As a founder-run company we're early in formal certification. We're honest about that: we hold ourselves to these standards today, and we'll publish our progress toward independent audits as we grow — the same way we publish our results.
