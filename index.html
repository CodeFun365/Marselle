Questions clinics ask

How is this different from Google Ads?

Google Ads rents attention — the moment you stop paying, the patients stop. Marselle grows what you own: your reputation, your Google presence, and the patients already in your records. That growth keeps compounding, and it's increasingly what AI assistants read when someone asks for a recommendation.

Will this take a lot of my time?

No. Your whole involvement is one text on Sunday and about ten minutes to approve the week's moves from your phone. Marselle does the rest.

What if AI writes something wrong to my patients?

Nothing reaches a patient without your approval — every message waits for your tap. And Marselle can't spend a dollar of your money; that capability doesn't exist in the system.

I already have an agency. Do I have to switch?

Keep them. Marselle is the layer between your agency and your patients: they keep running ads, website and brand; Marselle runs the organic growth and retention they don't, and honestly measures the whole picture — including their work — for less than a fifth of what you pay them. No turf war; one system per job.

Should I cut my ad budget and move it to Marselle?

Only if your own numbers say so — and we'll show you either way. Marselle reports honestly on what every channel returns, including your ads. If your ads are working, we'll say so plainly; if a chunk is quietly wasted, you'll see exactly how much and can decide where it's better spent. We never push you to cut anything — the scoreboard just makes the honest choice obvious.

Do I have to commit to everything at once?

No — and that's the point. Marselle starts with one workflow, usually reactivating your lapsed patients, because it's the fastest, highest-return win. We prove it in 30 days before anything expands. Only once a win lands do the rest — reviews, newsletter, Google posts, competitor watch — roll in, each earning its place. You're never buying a big platform on faith; you're watching it earn the next step, one result at a time. And the price never changes as it does more.
